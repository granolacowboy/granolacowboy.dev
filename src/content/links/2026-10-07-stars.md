A curated snapshot of my GitHub Lists: 2635 unique repositories across 28 public lists, rendered into a browsable research library. Selection is manual (I decide what belongs in each list); the rendering, the ordering by star count, and the metadata are mechanical and point-in-time. It is a working library, not a ranking or a recommendation engine, and inclusion is not endorsement: the lists hold tools I am evaluating, comparing, monitoring, or may want to revisit.
